Fixes in 0.5.1:
• PCAP-over-IP: other apps on the phone can no longer read the stream; a client allowlist is required on Wi-Fi.
• WireGuard fail-closed no longer lets IPv6 bypass an IPv4-only tunnel; no DNS leak after a SOCKS5 outage.
• TAXII URL indicators no longer block whole platforms such as GitHub.
• SIEM export no longer loses records silently.
• The app is now translatable.
